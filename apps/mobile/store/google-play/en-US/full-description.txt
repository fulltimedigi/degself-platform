DEGSELF helps you find car garages, workshops, and automotive services across Kuwait.

Search by workshop name, service, or area and review available business details and ratings before getting in touch.

Key features:
• Search automotive workshops across Kuwait.
• View specialty, location, address, rating, and available review count.
• Call or open WhatsApp when a workshop phone number is available.
• Open the workshop location and directions in Google Maps.
• Save favorites locally without creating an account.
• Optionally sign in to sync favorites across devices.
• Delete your account and associated data directly in the app.

You can browse and use the core directory features without creating an account. Always contact the workshop to confirm hours, services, and prices before visiting.

DEGSELF does not perform repairs and does not guarantee the work of listed businesses. The app helps users discover and contact them.
